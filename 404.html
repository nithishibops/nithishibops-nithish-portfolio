MY ANALYTICS WORKFLOW

How I build analytics solutions.

A structured process that begins with the business requirement and ends with a validated dashboard or reporting solution.

  1. 01

    Business Requirement

    Clarify the operational question, users, decisions and reporting need.

  2. 02

    Data Collection

    Identify the relevant operational, financial or reporting data required.

  3. 03

    Data Cleaning

    Check structure, completeness, formats, duplicates and data-quality issues.

  4. 04

    Data Modelling

    Organize tables and relationships so measures and analysis remain consistent.

  5. 05

    KPI / DAX Development

    Define measures and KPIs that answer the agreed business questions.

  6. 06

    Dashboard Design

    Build a readable view with clear hierarchy, filters and management context.

  7. 07

    Business Insights

    Interpret patterns, trends, exceptions and operational performance.

  8. 08

    Deployment / Presentation

    Validate outputs, publish the solution and present the logic clearly.

AI-assisted productivity, not AI-led positioning.

Where appropriate, AI-assisted tools may accelerate implementation or presentation. The portfolio emphasizes business requirements, data structure, KPI logic, validation, analytical thinking and the ability to explain the work.
